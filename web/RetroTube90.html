Snapshots

    
Long-press an image (or right-click it) to save it to your phone.

    
    

  


  

    
Tap Insert tape and pick a movie from your phone (MP4 or MOV), or a .vhs tape made by the desktop app. The VCR records it, threads the tape and presses play. Tap the screen to play or pause.

    
Snapshot tour runs the tape through play, pause, search, tracking, noise and the VCR screens and makes a contact sheet. Snapshot grabs the screen as it is.

    
Keyboard: Space play/pause, S stop, ←/→ rewind/fast-forward, T tracking, N noise, F fullscreen, F1 debug, Esc eject, I interlace, P power, L test tape. You can also drag a file onto the TV.
