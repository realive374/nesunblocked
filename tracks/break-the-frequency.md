## Genre blend

The three requested styles are carried by distinct elements rather than
blended into mush:

- **Nu metal** — downtuned 7-string guitar, syncopated palm-muted chugs,
  hip-hop swing in the drums, half-rapped verses, turntable scratches.
- **Funk metal** — slap-and-pop bass carrying the main groove, wah-wah
  guitar licks, 16th-note syncopation, snappy dry kit.
- **Alt metal** — the wide melodic chorus, atmospheric synth pad
  underneath, and the quiet/loud dynamic swing into the breakdown.
