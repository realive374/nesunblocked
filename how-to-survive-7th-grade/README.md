# How to Survive 7th Grade

A funny middle-grade survival novel (reading level grades 2–7) by **Jacob Garcia**.

The story follows Milo Pipkin through the chaos of seventh grade — a locker with
a mind of its own, mystery-meat cafeteria drama, dodgeball, homework avalanches,
a group project, a first crush, an alien-ish substitute, Picture Day, a science-
fair explosion, a best-friend fight, and a final boss math test — and sneaks a
**real, accurate fact into every single chapter** so kids learn while they laugh.

## Files

- `How_to_Survive_7th_Grade.docx` — the finished book.
  - **Body text:** Times New Roman
  - **Title & chapter headings:** Arial
  - Title page credits *By Jacob Garcia*
- `build-book.js` — the script that generates the `.docx`.

## Regenerate the document

```bash
npm install docx
node build-book.js
```

## The facts, chapter by chapter

1. **The Locker That Ate My Arm** — a 40-number, 3-turn lock has 40 × 40 × 40 = 64,000 combinations.
2. **Mystery Meat and Other Cafeteria Crimes** — honey never spoils; 3,000-year-old honey found in Egyptian tombs was still edible.
3. **Dodgeball Is Basically Legal Warfare** — your heart beats ~100,000 times a day; the peregrine falcon dives 200+ mph.
4. **The Homework Avalanche** — sleep files memories into long-term storage; spaced repetition beats cramming; the brain uses ~20% of your energy.
5. **Group Project of Doom** — oceans cover ~71% of Earth; the blue whale is the largest animal ever, with a heart the size of a small car.
6. **My Heart Did a Backflip** — blushing and "butterflies" are both caused by adrenaline, and you can't control them.
7. **The Substitute From Outer Space** — a day on Venus is longer than its year; ~1,300 Earths fit in Jupiter; sunlight is ~8 minutes old; space is silent.
8. **Picture Day: A Tragedy in One Photo** — "cowlick" and "photograph" (drawing with light) etymologies; no two fingerprints are alike, even in twins.
9. **The Science Fair Explosion** — baking soda + vinegar makes carbon-dioxide gas; Olympus Mons on Mars is the solar system's biggest volcano.
10. **When Best Friends Go to War** — dolphins have "signature whistles" (names); cows have best friends and get stressed when separated.
11. **The Big Test (Dun Dun Dunnn)** — the nine-times finger trick, PEMDAS, and the memory-palace technique.
12. **How to Actually Survive 7th Grade** — neuroplasticity: your brain builds new connections your whole life, so you can always improve.
